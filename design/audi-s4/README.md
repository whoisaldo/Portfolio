# Aldo's S4

The supplied `2013-audi-s4.zip` contains `source/FINAL_MODEL_S4_13.zip`. Its FBX and textures are preserved in `source/`. The body, cabin, lamp surfaces and UVs come from that model. Blender MCP was used to import, customize, inspect, render and export the car.

`ali-s4.blend` is the editable scene with packed textures and studio lighting. `scripts/blender/build_s4.py` rebuilds it from the original FBX. `public/models/ali-s4.glb` is the compressed browser asset. The transparent poster comes from a render of the same car.

## Reference choices

- Confirmed by the owner: 2013 B8.5 S4, 20-inch R8 wheels, 255/35 R20 tires, ECS brakes and suspension, RS4 front bumper, carbon trunk spoiler and rear diffuser, rain guards, AWE quad exhaust. The rear plate reads `CHOOM` at the owner's request.
- Front bumper: the petrol-station photograph `7ABC8DDB-1A6E-44F0-9899-656AF2BF7271.JPG`. The older open-hood image has a different bumper.
- Wheels and brakes: `IMG_0781.HEIC` and `IMG_0782.HEIC`.
- Rear: `FA349BA2-216A-4263-A0A6-255E210832DC.JPG` and `EA6892D2-1CDB-436B-9DF4-F285528AF53A.JPG`.
- Tire diameter is 686.5 mm, calculated from the owner's tire size. Wheelbase is 2.811 m, retained from the supplied model, consistent with the [Audi 2013 S4 specification sheet](https://www.audiworld.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/13S4_specs.pdf).

This is a reconstruction from photographs, not a scan. Paint is a visual grey match; the paint code, wheel offsets, exact ride height, tint percentage and aftermarket panel measurements remain unconfirmed. The spoke profiles and carbon parts are modeled from the available views. The cabin retains the supplied model's simplified geometry.

The hood separates along the source mesh's existing panel seam and rotates on its rear hinge. Its telescoping gas strut stays attached during movement. The visible engine bay follows `audi-s4-engine-bay.jpg`, including the V6 TFSI cover, exposed supercharger housing, APR carbon intake, red coil packs, reservoirs and plumbing. These are modeled visual approximations; internal engine parts are not represented. `scripts/blender/hood_and_details.py` contains the hood, engine bay, red S badge and grille seam corrections.

## Rebuild

On this Mac, use the Python environment that already has the MCP SDK:

```sh
/Users/aldo/.local/share/uv/tools/mcp-for-blender/bin/python scripts/blender/run_mcp.py --server /Users/aldo/.local/bin/blender-mcp
npm run car:assets
npm run check:s4
```

The build creates only its own `Ali_S4_Final` scene. It does not clear other Blender scenes. The saved `.blend` contains the S4 scene and its dependencies. Review PNGs and the uncompressed GLB are local build artifacts, excluded from Git. No reference photograph is published by the model pipeline.

The optimization uses [glTF Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev/) to deduplicate and weld vertices, then apply Meshopt compression. It preserves named wheel and steering pivots. No geometric simplification is applied. Texture UVs outside 0–1 are intentional for the repeating carbon weave.

## Web integration

- `src/three/car/object.js` shares the GLB download, with independent disposable instances for each viewer.
- Asset URLs include a content hash, regenerated by `car:assets`, so geometry revisions replace cached models and posters.
- `src/lib/drift.js` preloads the geometry along with the intro's scene module. The transparent S4 render replaces the old flat fallback.
- `src/components/GarageModel.jsx` loads WebGL only after the visitor chooses Explore in 3D. Camera presets, mouse/touch orbit, keyboard rotation, zoom, Open/Close hood and retry are available. Opening the hood frames the engine bay. The short hinge motion respects reduced-motion preferences. The scene renders on input, during the hinge transition and on resize, and pauses outside the viewport or when the tab is hidden.
- The original Front, Engine bay and Rear photographs and part markers remain available in the Garage tabs.

Coordinate convention in the exported GLB: metres, +Y up, +Z forward, +X vehicle left. `steer_fl` and `steer_fr` turn about Y. Their child wheel groups spin about X. The rear wheels have their own fixed axle parents. `check:s4` decodes the compressed file and verifies the hierarchy, tire radius, wheelbase, finite geometry, embedded textures, custom plate, spoiler clearance and exhaust placement within the bumper apertures.
